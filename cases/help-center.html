Edrone · Self-Service & Product Education

Turning a Help Center into a scalable self-service experience.

During a major product evolution, I became the Brazilian focal point for restructuring and standardizing a large Help Center — treating documentation as part of the customer experience, not just a writing task.

~300 articles~2 monthsBrazil + PolandContent UX

Context

The product was going through a significant redesign and new-feature cycle. Help Center content had grown organically across multiple contributors, creating inconsistencies in tone, terminology, structure, formatting, and organization.

The challenge

Customers needed reliable answers while the product itself was changing. The content system also needed enough consistency to remain maintainable after the transformation.

My role

I was the Brazilian focal point for the transformation. I reviewed and updated approximately 300 articles in roughly two months, aligned terminology and content patterns, supported other contributors, and collaborated across Brazil and Poland.

The insight

Documentation only works as self-service when it matches how customers look for help. Clarity was not enough; discoverability mattered too.

Approach

01 · AuditIdentify inconsistencies created by organic growth and product changes.
02 · StandardizeCreate repeatable structures, terminology, and writing patterns.
03 · Design for searchConsider customer questions, keywords, and support language.
04 · ScaleGuide contributors and align tutorial conventions across markets.

Design decisions

Search behavior over internal vocabulary

Content was structured with the words customers were likely to use when looking for an answer.

Patterns over one-off fixes

Standardization made the content easier to scan and gave future contributors a clearer model to follow.

One experience across formats

Tutorial videos were brought closer to a shared visual and instructional standard.

Impact & scale

~300articles reviewed / updated
~2 monthsdelivery window
2 marketsBrazil + Poland alignment

No verified product-adoption or support-deflection metric is available, so this case does not claim one.

What I learned

Self-service content is part of the product experience: if users cannot find, understand, and act on an answer, the documentation has not finished its job.

Public context

The Help Center has continued to evolve since this project. The current public environment is linked only as context and should not be interpreted as a claim that its present-day state is entirely my work.
