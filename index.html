About

From engineering systems to engineering adoption.

My background is in Biomedical Engineering, where understanding technology, people, processes, and real-world constraints was part of the job.

Over time, my work moved through healthcare and medical technology into Customer Success, Customer Education, Product Education, Learning Experience Design, and SaaS.

What connects those experiences is the same problem: making something complex easier for someone else to understand, use, and get value from.

Today, I design learning and enablement experiences across the customer and product journey — from onboarding and self-service to interactive learning, product education, and AI-enabled experiences.
