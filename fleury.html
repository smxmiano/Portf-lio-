Grupo Fleury · Behavior Change

Designing a learning campaign to turn awareness into behavior change.

A broad privacy-education brief became a measurable, two-month behavior-change campaign combining storytelling, weekly communication, and repeated observation.

Healthcare200+ employees2-month challengeMeasurement

Context

As Brazil's data-protection law was being introduced, a healthcare HR environment handled large volumes of sensitive employee information and printed documents. The brief was broad: educate employees and encourage better handling of sensitive information.

The challenge

Awareness alone would not show whether behavior had changed. The initiative needed a way to make an abstract compliance topic concrete, memorable, and measurable.

My role

I designed the campaign, created the animated narrative and supporting presentation, established a baseline, launched the initiative to 200+ employees, measured the same indicators weekly, and created the ongoing communication.

The insight

If the goal is behavior change, the experience needs more than a one-time explanation. It needs reinforcement and observable signals.

Approach

01 · Define behaviorMeasure printing volume, sensitive information in the sample, and documents left near the printing area.
02 · Make it understandableUse characters and storytelling to introduce privacy and responsible document handling.
03 · ReinforcePublish weekly progress communication plus a serialized mystery/comic narrative.
04 · MeasureReturn every Friday for two months and track the same indicators.

Impact

The project generated measurable changes in monitored behaviors and was selected as a Top 10 finalist in an internal innovation and resource-optimization recognition program.

Exact reduction percentages are intentionally omitted until the original measurement materials are fully validated.

What I learned

Learning is more powerful when the desired behavior is defined before the content — and when communication continues long enough to reinforce the change.
